### Fixed: T61 affordability pre-check disables empty infinite wallets (medium)

Previously `SimWorld::spend` accepted an infinite wallet even when its displayed balance was zero. T61's new balance-only pre-check returned before `spend` could apply that rule. It could also incorrectly charge a finite fallback when the own-currency wallet was infinite.

`battle_root.gd` now adapts infinite-wallet balances before calling the single `UnitDefs.placement_plan` rule, shared by placement and preview. Tests prove zero-land/infinite-land Spearman placement and preview, no resource deduction, and infinite-sea priority for Signal Battery placed on land while finite land has 20. All three assertions failed before the fix and pass afterward. No native API or normal economy changes. The pre-existing failed-spawn refund-to-own-wallet quirk remains deliberately unchanged, as T61's brief requires an owner decision.
